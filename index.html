    ABOUT US

    From Loom to Legacy: The Story of Mina Tricots.
    Before Mina Tricots became a brand, it was simply our family's way of surviving. Deep in the heart of the Atlas Mountains, in a small Amazigh village where resources were scarce but heritage ran deep, our family had only two things to rely on: unyielding resilience and a modest wooden loom passed down through generations. Money was tight, winter winds were unforgiving, and opportunities were hard to come by. Driven by necessity, our parents began crafting clothing right from our home. Every piece was made by hand—spun from raw wool, woven with patient care, and stitched with purpose. We didn't just make garments to stay warm; we made them to live. When you wear Mina Tricots, you aren't just wearing a shirt—you are wearing a story of heritage, survival, and hard-earned success.
